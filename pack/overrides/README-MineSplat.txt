MineSplat is a client-only mod.

Open a world, start your TripoSplat API, then press M + G.
Schematics are written to schematics/minesplat/.

Optional Chisels & Bits output:
- In Prism Edit Instance -> Mods -> Download Mods, install Chisels & Bits
  21.1.33.
- Select "Chisels & Bits miniature" in MineSplat.
- Placement is available in Creative singleplayer.
- Saved blueprints are stored in minesplat/blueprints/.
- MineSplat and Litematica continue to work normally without Chisels & Bits.
